PUT YOUR GAMEPLAY BACKGROUND CLIPS IN THIS FOLDER.

Shortsmith plays gameplay footage (e.g. Minecraft parkour, Subway Surfers) behind
every video. It is NOT included in the download — you add your own:

  - 3 to 5 clips is ideal; 5+ minutes each is best.
  - Formats: .mp4  .mkv  .webm  .mov
  - Record your OWN gameplay (safest — zero copyright questions), or download
    clips whose creator explicitly allows reuse ("no copyright ... free to use").

Every time a video is made, Shortsmith picks a RANDOM clip and a RANDOM section of
it, then auto-crops it to vertical 9:16 — so your uploads never look identical.

If this folder is empty, videos still render, but with an abstract animated colour
background instead of gameplay.
